# Practical AI Quiz Emails

A 5-email sales funnel designed to drive prospects through two key assessments:
1. **CEO Sales 60-Second Quiz** (Email 1)
2. **Practical AI IQ Quiz** (Emails 2-5)

## Funnel Flow

| Email | Purpose | Target Quiz | Key Hook |
|-------|---------|------------|----------|
| Email 1 | Hook & Initial Assessment | CEO Sales 60-Second Quiz | CEO-ready sales skills assessment |
| Email 2 | Practical Value Demo | Practical AI IQ Quiz | Real-world AI scenarios |
| Email 3 | Framework & Education | Practical AI IQ Quiz | Storyselling + AI framework |
| Email 4 | Social Proof | Practical AI IQ Quiz | 3,000+ professionals, 73% learned something |
| Email 5 | Final CTA | Practical AI IQ Quiz | Direct ask with options |

## Quiz Links

- **CEO Sales Quiz**: https://ceo-sales-60-second-quiz-outreach.netlify.app/
- **Practical AI IQ Quiz**: https://practical-ai-skills-iq.netlify.app/

## Assets

- `assets/emails/` - All 5 email templates (HTML)
- **New emails** (Quiz-focused): `Email_X_*_Quiz.html`
- **Original emails** (Speaking-focused): `Email_X_*.html` (archived for reference)

## Design Language

- **Primary Color**: Gold (#C9A84C)
- **Accent Color**: Dark Navy (#1a1a1a)
- **Hover State**: Darker Gold (#b8962f)
- **Background**: Cream/Light (#FBF5E3)
- **Font**: System stack (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif)
- **Serif Accent**: Georgia (headlines)

## How to Use

1. Open any email template in `assets/emails/`
2. Replace `[Contact Name]` with prospect name
3. Replace `[Org Name]` where applicable
4. Customize date and sender signature as needed
5. Send via your email platform (HubSpot, Mailchimp, etc.)

## Email Structure

Each email maintains:
- Professional gradient header with headshot
- Research hook or compelling statistic
- About Scott section
- Value proposition
- Visual hierarchy with highlighted boxes
- Clear CTA buttons
- Professional footer with signature

## Customization Tips

- Adjust the 90-second/60-second copy if quiz duration changes
- Update statistics as quiz data updates
- Testimonials can be swapped with real customer feedback
- P.S. lines are intentionally conversational and non-salesy
