# Practical AI Quiz Emails — Project Overview

## What Is This?

A 5-email sales funnel designed to drive **qualified prospects** into two key quiz assessments:

1. **Email 1** → CEO Sales 60-Second Quiz
2. **Emails 2-5** → Practical AI IQ Quiz

The funnel is built to maximize engagement through research-backed hooks, compelling value propositions, social proof, and clear calls-to-action.

## Why It Exists

**Goal**: Create a systematic way to identify and segment prospects based on their AI sales readiness and practical AI skills, leading to qualified leads for future sales conversations or training programs.

**Previous approach**: 5-email funnel designed to book speaking engagements.  
**Current approach**: Pivot to quiz-based assessment funnel for lead qualification and skill identification.
