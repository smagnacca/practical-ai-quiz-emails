# Funnel Strategy — 5-Email Quiz Flow

## Funnel Progression

```
Email 1: HOOK
   ↓
Email 2: VALUE DEMO
   ↓
Email 3: EDUCATION + FRAMEWORK
   ↓
Email 4: SOCIAL PROOF
   ↓
Email 5: FINAL CTA
```

---

## Email 1: CEO Sales Assessment (Quiz Hook)

**File**: Email_1_Quiz_Hook.html  
**Target Quiz**: CEO Sales 60-Second Quiz  
**Optimal Send**: First email in sequence

### Psychology
- Opens with a **powerful stat** ("CEOs closing 40% more deals")
- Frames assessment as a **competitive advantage tool**, not a sales pitch
- Emphasizes **speed** (60 seconds) and **honesty** (real assessment, not marketing)

### Hook
> "CEOs and sales leaders who assess their AI-selling readiness close 40% more deals this year."

### Key Benefits Highlighted
- Real-world scenarios
- Instant insights
- No time waste

### CTA
"Take the 60-Second Assessment"

### Why It Works
- Opens with Scott's credibility (Babson, Harvard, 20+ years)
- Immediately establishes that this is NOT a sales pitch
- Makes taking the quiz feel like a smart business decision, not a lead magnet

## Email 2: Practical AI Skills (Value Demo)

**File**: Email_2_Practical_AI_Quiz.html  
**Target Quiz**: Practical AI IQ Quiz  
**Optimal Send**: 2-3 days after Email 1

### Psychology
- Shifts from **CEO sales** to **practical AI skills**
- Introduces the concept of **hidden blind spots**
- Uses case study format (Insight → Action → Result)

### Hook
> "Professionals who assess their AI skills and act on what they learn earn a 30% productivity boost within the first quarter."

### Key Insight
"Most people think they know how to use AI. This quiz shows you the truth. And the truth is usually surprising — in a good way."

### CTA
"Take the Practical AI IQ Quiz"

### Why It Works
- Moves from **speed** (Email 1) to **depth** (Email 2)
- Introduces accountability ("and act on what they learn")
- Frames the quiz as a **productivity tool**, not just an assessment

## Email 3: Storyselling + Framework (Education)

**File**: Email_3_Framework_Quiz.html  
**Target Quiz**: Practical AI IQ Quiz  
**Optimal Send**: 2-3 days after Email 2

### Psychology
- **Educates** the prospect on a concrete framework
- Ties framework directly to the quiz content
- Builds **authority** through Stanford research

### Hook
> "People are 22x more likely to remember facts told in a story than facts told in isolation."

### The Three Skills (Framework)
1. **Curiosity** — Use AI to find signal, ask human questions
2. **Lifelong Learning** — Learn fast, apply, teach others
3. **Agility & Adaptability** — Tell different stories to different people

### Connection to Quiz
"The Practical AI IQ quiz measures exactly these three skills in real-world scenarios. Your score will show you where your edge is."

### CTA
"Take the Practical AI IQ Quiz"

### Why It Works
- Provides **educational value** before the ask
- Explains **what the quiz measures** (removes mystery)
- Uses Stanford research as proof point
- Frames skills as **learnable**, not innate

## Email 4: Social Proof (Confidence Building)

**File**: Email_4_Social_Proof_Quiz.html  
**Target Quiz**: Practical AI IQ Quiz  
**Optimal Send**: 2-3 days after Email 3

### Psychology
- Introduces **social proof** (3,000+ professionals)
- Normalizes taking the quiz ("Everyone learns something")
- Includes **authentic testimonials**

### Key Stats
- 3,000+ professionals have taken the quiz
- 73% report learning something surprising

### Testimonials (3 examples)
1. VP of Sales, Fortune 500: "I wasn't as far along as I thought"
2. Sales Director, Tech: "Changed how we prioritize AI training"
3. Sales Executive, Insurance: "Felt real, not generic"

### CTA
"Take the Practical AI IQ Quiz" / "Let's Talk"

### Why It Works
- **Removes risk** ("3,000 others have done this")
- **Normalizes the discovery** ("73% learned something surprising")
- Testimonials are **specific** (roles, industries) without being fake-sounding
- Multiple CTAs (quiz or conversation)

## Email 5: Final CTA (Decision Time)

**File**: Email_5_Final_CTA_Quiz.html  
**Target Quiz**: Practical AI IQ Quiz  
**Optimal Send**: 2-3 days after Email 4

### Psychology
- **Creates urgency** (implicit: "assessors move ahead, non-assessors stay stuck")
- Removes friction with **three options** (take quiz, ask questions, get more info)
- Acknowledges that the prospect may not be ready yet

### Hook
> "The professionals who assess their AI skills take action. The ones who don't stay stuck. Which one are you?"

### Three Options Presented
1. **Take the Quiz Now** — 90 seconds, instant insights
2. **Ask Questions** — Not sure? Reply and ask
3. **Get More Info** — Want details before committing? We'll send it

### Key Message
"Every professional I know who took this quiz learned something. Most learned something that changed how they approach AI."

### CTA
"Take the Practical AI IQ Quiz" / "Contact Me"

### Why It Works
- **Direct**, no fluff
- Acknowledges **different readiness levels** (quiz now vs. get more info)
- Removes excuse to procrastinate ("You have 3 paths forward")
- No-pressure P.S. ("Don't wait, but also no pressure")

## Optimal Send Timing

```
Day 1: Email 1 (CEO Sales Hook)
Day 4: Email 2 (Practical AI Value)
Day 7: Email 3 (Framework Education)
Day 10: Email 4 (Social Proof)
Day 13: Email 5 (Final CTA)
```

**Rationale**: 2-3 days between emails allows time for reading, thinking, and building anticipation without losing momentum.

## What Each Email Assumes the Prospect Knows

- **Email 1**: Assumes they're a CEO/sales leader interested in AI
- **Email 2**: Assumes they've read Email 1 (no need to re-introduce Scott)
- **Email 3**: Assumes they understand the value of assessment
- **Email 4**: Assumes they're considering it (building confidence)
- **Email 5**: Assumes they need a final push or permission to say "no"

## Why This Sequence Works

1. **Email 1** establishes credibility and speed (no time to think)
2. **Email 2** introduces value and builds curiosity
3. **Email 3** educates and explains what's being measured
4. **Email 4** removes risk with social proof
5. **Email 5** provides choice and removes decision friction

Each email **builds on the last** without repeating information. By Email 5, the prospect has heard:
- Why assessment matters (Email 1)
- What it reveals (Email 2)
- What skills are being measured (Email 3)
- That thousands of others found value (Email 4)
- That they can take action now or learn more (Email 5)
