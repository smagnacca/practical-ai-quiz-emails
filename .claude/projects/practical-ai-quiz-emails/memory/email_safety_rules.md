# Email Safety Rules — RULE ZERO-ZERO (IMMUTABLE)

## Why This Rule Exists

**Scott sent quiz emails to 14 cold professional contacts (Harvard, Babson, NAIFA, YPO, LIMRA) who had never taken a quiz. He had to send personal apologies. This must never happen again.**

This rule is immutable. It applies to every email project, every session, every send.

---

## Before ANY Email Send to Real Contacts — ALL THREE Required

### 1️⃣ Test Send to scott.magnacca1@gmail.com ONLY (First)

- Send the exact email you plan to send to your real list
- Scott reads it in his inbox and confirms:
  - Content looks right
  - Links work (both quiz URLs)
  - Formatting is correct across different email clients
  - Personalization works correctly ([Contact Name] replaces properly)
- Only after Scott approves the test send do you proceed to step 2

**No exceptions. Even if the code "looks perfect," test to self first.**

### 2️⃣ Audience Verification Out Loud

State explicitly to Scott:

> "These [N] contacts are [quiz takers / event registrants / opted-in leads]. The email content matches because [reason]."

Examples of VALID audiences:
- ✅ "These 50 contacts already took the CEO Sales quiz — this is Email 2 followup"
- ✅ "These 30 are event registrants from the Practical AI workshop — they opted in"
- ✅ "These 15 are prior course graduates who requested more content"

Examples of INVALID audiences:
- ❌ "These are cold leads I found on LinkedIn" (no opt-in)
- ❌ "These are Harvard alumni I thought might be interested" (didn't ask them)
- ❌ "These people match the profile of our ideal customer" (doesn't matter; no consent)

**If you cannot complete that sentence with confidence, do not send.**

### 3️⃣ Scott Explicitly Approves the Recipient List

Show Scott:
1. The email content (screenshot or preview)
2. A sample of 5-10 recipient names
3. The total count ("N people total")
4. Ask: **"Ready to send to these N people?"**

Wait for explicit "yes" or "approved." Do NOT assume approval based on earlier conversation.

## What "Testing" Means for Email Projects

| Approach | Status | Correct? |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Send to scott.magnacca1@gmail.com, review, approve | ✅ Valid test | YES |
| Fire against 50 real leads database "to see if it works" | ❌ Not a test | NO |
| Send to a small sample (10 people) to "test first" | ❌ Not a test | NO |
| Preview in email builder, code looks good, send to 100 people | ❌ Not a test | NO |

**Test = send to Scott's email only. Real send = after test approval + audience verification + explicit approval.**

## Red Flags (Stop & Ask Before Sending)

🚩 "These are people who might be interested"  
→ They haven't opted in. Don't send.

🚩 "The CEO quiz might be relevant to this list"  
→ Relevance ≠ consent. Don't send.

🚩 "I found these emails and they match our ICP"  
→ Still not opted in. Don't send.

🚩 "They'll probably like this"  
→ Doesn't matter. They didn't ask for it. Don't send.

🚩 "It's just a quiz, not a hard sell"  
→ Still need opt-in. Don't send.

## Examples of VALID Sends

1. **Quiz follow-up**: "These 40 people completed the CEO Sales quiz last month. Email 2 is the Practical AI IQ quiz they didn't take yet."  
   → Valid because they already engaged

2. **Event registrants**: "These 25 people registered for the Practical AI workshop. Email 1 introduces the quiz they'll take in the workshop."  
   → Valid because they explicitly registered

3. **Course graduates**: "These 12 people completed the AI-Enabled Selling course. Email 1 introduces the certification quiz."  
   → Valid because they finished the course

4. **Opt-in list**: "These 60 people joined the AI Sales Newsletter. Email 1 is month 3 of the curriculum they subscribed to."  
   → Valid because they explicitly subscribed

---

## Examples of INVALID Sends

1. **Cold LinkedIn outreach**: "Found 100 CTOs on LinkedIn. Sent them the CEO Sales quiz email."  
   → Invalid: no opt-in, no prior contact

2. **Company domain matching**: "Found 50 people at Fortune 500 companies. Sent them the Practical AI quiz email."  
   → Invalid: matching profile ≠ consent

3. **Educational assumption**: "These are sales leaders. They probably want to know about the quiz."  
   → Invalid: assumption ≠ consent

4. **Purchased list**: "Bought a list of 200 B2B sales professionals. Sent them Email 1."  
   → Invalid: purchased lists often lack proper consent

## What If Scott Approves in a Different Way?

**If Scott says:** "Yeah, just send it"  
**What you should do:** Stop and ask explicitly  
- "Just to confirm — I'm sending [email name] to [N] contacts who are [reason]. Scott approves? Yes?"

**If Scott says:** "I think these are fine"  
**What you should do:** Clarify  
- "So I should send to all [N] of them? You approve?"

**If Scott says:** "Why do you keep asking?"  
**What you should do:** Remind him of Rule Zero-Zero  
- "Rule Zero-Zero requires explicit approval before any real send (we learned this from the prior incident). I need a clear 'yes' to proceed."
