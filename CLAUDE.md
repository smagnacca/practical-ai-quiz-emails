## Funnel Logic

3. **Email 3** — Educate on framework (storyselling + AI) and explain what the quiz measures

**Optimal spacing**: 2-3 days between emails for maximum engagement.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I reuse these emails for different audiences?**
A: Yes, but test with a small segment first. Adjust hooks based on audience industry/role.

**Q: What if someone completes Email 1 quiz but doesn't move to Email 2?**
A: Add them to a "warm lead" segment. Email 2 should be triggered by quiz completion, not time-based.

**Q: Can I A/B test subject lines?**
A: Yes. The current subjects are strong, but test variations of the hooks (e.g., "Measure Your AI Edge" vs. "Know Your Competitive Advantage").

**Q: How do I know if the quiz links are working?**
A: Always test send to yourself first. Click both quiz links and verify the page loads correctly.
